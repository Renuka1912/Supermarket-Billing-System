# Staff Guide: Using Customer Contact in Billing

## Quick Start

### Option 1: Billing a Registered Customer

1. **Search for Customer** (Optional but easier)
   - In "Customer Information" section, type customer name or phone
   - Select from the dropdown list: `↓` arrow shows all customers

2. **Auto-Filled Phone**
   - Once you select a customer, their phone number auto-fills
   - You can edit it if the customer gives a different number
   - The selected customer is confirmed with a green checkmark

3. **Complete the Sale**
   - Add products to cart
   - Choose payment mode
   - Click "Complete Sale & Print"

### Option 2: Billing a Walk-in Customer (No Record)

1. **Leave Customer Search Empty**
   - Don't search for or select any customer

2. **Enter Contact Number**
   - Manually type the customer's phone number in the "Customer contact number" field
   - This is **REQUIRED** - you cannot complete sale without it

3. **Complete the Sale**
   - Add products to cart
   - Choose payment mode  
   - Click "Complete Sale & Print"

### Option 3: Quick Override

1. **Select Customer as Normal**
   - Phone auto-fills from their record

2. **Edit Contact Number**
   - If customer provides different number, just edit the field
   - Use the edited number for billing

3. **Complete Sale**

---

## Important Points

⚠️ **Contact Number is REQUIRED**
- Every sale must have a contact number
- Get phone if customer doesn't have it (or use alternate contact)
- Cannot proceed without it

✓ **Green Checkmark**
- Means customer info is confirmed
- "Customer Information" section turns green

📞 **What If Customer Has No Phone?**
- Get any contact number (WhatsApp, business phone, etc.)
- Use that for the record
- Or ask manager for guidance

## Checking Sales History

Go to **Sales History** page to see:
- Customers' names
- Their contact numbers (in dedicated column)
- All transaction details
- Previous sales with each customer

Click **View** on any sale to see full details including contact number.

## FAQ

**Q: What if I select a customer but want to use a different number?**
A: Just edit the phone field after selection. Your edit will be used.

**Q: Is the contact number used to update the customer record?**
A: No. The number is only saved with this specific sale. Customer records are unchanged.

**Q: What if a walk-in customer buys multiple times?**
A: Each sale is independent. They appear as separate "Walk-in Customer" records unless you create a customer profile (Customers → Add New).

**Q: Can I complete sale without entering contact?**
A: No. System requires contact number and will show an error.

**Q: Where can I see all sales with a specific customer?**
A: Go to Sales History → Search by name or phone → You'll see their transactions.

## Tips for Best Results

1. **Keep it consistent** - If customer has been billed before, select them from history
2. **Accurate entry** - Double-check phone numbers, especially for WhatsApp
3. **Alternate contacts** - If no phone, use email or any unique identifier
4. **Use dropdown** - Click `↓` to see all customers instead of typing
5. **Review receipt** - Check that contact is printed correctly
6. **Sales history** - Regularly check to verify customer info was saved

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| "Enter customer contact number" error | Fill the contact field before clicking Complete |
| Customer name not auto-filling | Make sure to click on the customer in dropdown |
| Wrong number was saved | Contact Admin - may need to correct sales record |
| Can't find customer | Click the `↓` arrow to see full customer list |
| New customer not appearing | Go to Customers page and add them first |
